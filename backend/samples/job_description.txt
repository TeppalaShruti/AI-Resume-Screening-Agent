Junior AI Research Associate
ROOMAN Technologies - Bengaluru (Hybrid)

About the role
We are hiring a Junior AI Research Associate to build and evaluate applied NLP and
LLM systems. You will prototype retrieval and reasoning pipelines, run experiments,
and ship research prototypes into production services with our engineering team.

Responsibilities
- Build NLP pipelines: document parsing, embeddings, semantic search and RAG
- Fine-tune and evaluate transformer models; report rigorous metrics
- Serve models behind Python REST APIs and collaborate on the React dashboards
- Document experiments and present findings to non-technical stakeholders

Required qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, AI, Data Science or a related field
- 1+ years of hands-on experience with Python for machine learning
- Strong practical knowledge of NLP, Transformers, embeddings and cosine similarity
- Experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow and scikit-learn
- Experience building REST APIs with FastAPI or Flask
- Comfortable with Git, Docker and Linux
- Solid statistics, model evaluation and feature engineering fundamentals

Preferred
- Exposure to LLMs, prompt engineering, RAG and vector databases
- Cloud deployment on AWS or GCP
- Research publications or strong open-source projects
- Data visualization and dashboarding, React or Streamlit
- Excellent communication, problem solving and teamwork
